Color Picker (<k-color-picker>)
Import: import 'kempo-ui/src/components/ColorPicker.js';
Docs: https://dustinpoissant.github.io/kempo-ui/components/color-picker.html

Full color picker (hex, rgb, hsl, hsv, alpha)

Details:
- `value` property
- `readonly` (Boolean): shows the color and still submits it; the text field is read-only, and the format select and swatch are disabled
- `disabled` (Boolean): the color cannot be changed and is left out of the form; a disabled fieldset disables it too

Calendar (<k-calendar>)
Import: import 'kempo-ui/src/components/Calendar.js';
Docs: https://dustinpoissant.github.io/kempo-ui/components/calendar.html

Inline month-grid date picker widget

Details:
- `mode="single"` (default) or `mode="range"`
- `value` is ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` (single) or comma-separated pair (range)
- `min`/`max` bounds
- form-associated
- fires `change`
- `readonly` (Boolean): shows the selected date and still submits it; days cannot be picked, but month and year navigation still works

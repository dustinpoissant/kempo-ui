Combobox (<k-combobox>)
Import: import 'kempo-ui/src/components/Combobox.js';
Docs: https://dustinpoissant.github.io/kempo-ui/components/combobox.html

Searchable dropdown with debounced `search` event, dynamic `setOptions()`, `searching` indicator, keyboard nav

Details:
- options are strings or `{label, value}` objects
- `readonly` (Boolean): shows the value and still submits it; the field cannot be typed in and the option list does not open

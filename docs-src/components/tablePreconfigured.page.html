Set the controls attribute to give a table a toolbar without slotting each control yourself. The toolbars are <k-control-bar>s, so on a narrow screen the controls collapse into a “more” menu instead of wrapping. The control modules are imported for you.

Anything you slot into the table’s top slot (or without a slot, for the bottom) replaces the preconfigured bar in that position, and controls="none" (the default) shows nothing. Apart from the hide button in full, row controls (edit, delete, open) are not part of any set; add them with the before and after slots.

ValueTop barBottom bar
none——
minimalSearch on the left; filters and fields (show / hide / order) on the right—
normalSearch, filters, fields, and Export CSV on the rightPrevious and next page on the left, page size on the right
fullSearch, filters, fields; Export CSV and JSON in the center; hidden count and show all on the right; a hide button on every rowFirst, previous, next and last on the left; page select in the center; page size on the right

The controls that matter most keep their place longest as the table narrows: the search has priority="2" and the page size and page select priority="1". The presets are meant for tables that show data: they have no selection, import, edit or delete controls, since those need handlers of your own (add them yourself with enable-selection, kc-tc-delete-selected and the row controls). full does add a kc-tc-hide button to every row, as hiding a record needs no code. Slot your own after controls to replace it. The page controls only do something with enable-pages.

Minimal

Find records and choose which fields are shown.

<k-table controls="minimal"></k-table>

Normal

The everyday toolbar: the minimal bar, an export, and paging.

<k-table controls="normal" enable-pages page-size="5" page-size-options="[5,10,25]"></k-table>

Full

Everything for browsing data: search, filters, fields, exports, hidden records and full paging.

<k-table controls="full" enable-pages page-size="5" page-size-options="[5,10,25]"></k-table>

Replacing a Bar

Slot your own controls in top to replace the preconfigured top bar while keeping the preconfigured bottom bar.

<k-table controls="normal" enable-pages page-size="5" page-size-options="[5,10,25]">
  <k-control-bar slot="top">
    <kc-tc-search></kc-tc-search>
    <kc-tc-export-json slot="right"></kc-tc-export-json>
  </k-control-bar>
</k-table>
